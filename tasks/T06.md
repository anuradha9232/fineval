# T06 - Anomaly detection

**Dataset:** `segment_quarterly.csv` | **Type:** open | **Rubric:** [`rubrics/T06.json`](../rubrics/T06.json)

## Prompt

> Looking at the quarterly revenue trends by sector, is there anything unusual in the data that I should look into? Point me to it and tell me how you'd think about it.

## Targets

One clear outlier exists. The model should find it, quantify it, and respond proportionately - not miss it, and not over-react by declaring the whole business in decline.

## Verified answer

- **Automobile Q4** is the anomaly: it fell to Rs 150 cr from Rs 215 cr, a **~30% drop**, while **every other sector grew** in Q4.
- Sensible response: flag it and recommend investigating the cause (one-off event? data error? demand shift?) before drawing conclusions - not a signal about the whole portfolio.
