# T05 — FY revenue and share table (spreadsheet output)

**Dataset:** `segment_quarterly.csv` · **Type:** spreadsheet · **Rubric:** [`rubrics/T05.json`](../rubrics/T05.json)

## Prompt

> From the quarterly data, build me a table with one row per sector showing total FY revenue (sum of the four quarters) and each sector's share of total FY revenue as a percentage. Sort it from highest revenue to lowest, and give me the overall total.

## Targets

A spreadsheet-style task: the output must be a sorted table, the **total must reconcile**, and the **shares must sum to ~100%**. Spot checks on individual sector totals catch selective errors.
