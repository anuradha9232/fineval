# T03 — Valuation drivers (open-ended)

**Dataset:** `company_financials.csv` · **Type:** open · **Rubric:** [`rubrics/T03.json`](../rubrics/T03.json)

## Prompt

> Based on what the numbers actually show, is market cap better explained by a company's revenue or by its profit? Explain your reasoning, and tell me one thing an investor should be careful about before treating this relationship as a rule.

## Targets

Open-ended prompts pull models toward confident over-interpretation — declaring causation, or treating a single sample as a universal law. There isn't one correct wording, so the rubric rewards a grounded conclusion and leans on negative criteria for the over-claims.

## Verified answer (grounded)

- Profit is the stronger predictor (0.972 vs 0.924), but **both** are strongly correlated.
- A valid caution: this is an **association, not proof of causation**, and the pattern may not hold for a different set of companies or time period.
