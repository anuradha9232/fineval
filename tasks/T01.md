# T01 — What drives market cap: revenue or profit?

**Dataset:** `company_financials.csv` · **Type:** closed · **Rubric:** [`rubrics/T01.json`](../rubrics/T01.json)

## Prompt

> I've attached a CSV of 120 listed companies with their sector, revenue, net profit, and market cap (all in ₹ crore). I'm trying to understand what actually moves market cap. Please tell me: (1) the average revenue across all the companies; (2) the Pearson correlation between revenue and market cap; (3) the Pearson correlation between net profit and market cap; and (4) which of the two — revenue or profit — market cap tracks more closely. Give me the actual numbers.

## Targets

Two similar correlations invite the model to compute one and assume the other, or to state the wrong one as stronger. The high-weight criteria are the two correlation values and the comparison.

## Verified answer

- Average revenue ≈ ₹1,435 crore
- Pearson (revenue vs market cap) = 0.924
- Pearson (profit vs market cap) = 0.972
- **Profit is the stronger predictor.**
