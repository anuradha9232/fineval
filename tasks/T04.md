# T04 — Quarter-on-quarter growth by sector

**Dataset:** `segment_quarterly.csv` · **Type:** closed · **Rubric:** [`rubrics/T04.json`](../rubrics/T04.json)

## Prompt

> The attached file has quarterly revenue by sector for FY2024 (Q1–Q4, ₹ crore). Work out the Q4-over-Q3 growth for each sector as a percentage, and tell me which sector grew the most and which grew the least.

## Targets

Growth must be a **percentage change** ((Q4 − Q3) / Q3), not an absolute rupee difference. A model that reports the raw difference can still rank sectors plausibly but gives the wrong magnitudes — caught by a negative criterion.

## Verified answer

| Sector | Q4-vs-Q3 growth |
|---|---|
| IT Services | **+8.51%** (highest) |
| Pharma | +8.20% |
| Energy | +6.94% |
| Banking | +6.17% |
| FMCG | +5.45% |
| Automobile | **-30.23%** (lowest — a decline) |
