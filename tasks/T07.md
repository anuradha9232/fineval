# T07 — Largest sector and revenue concentration

**Dataset:** `segment_quarterly.csv` · **Type:** closed · **Rubric:** [`rubrics/T07.json`](../rubrics/T07.json)

## Prompt

> Which sector contributes the most to full-year revenue, how much is it in rupees and as a share of the total, and roughly how concentrated is the revenue in the top couple of sectors?

## Targets

Tests aggregation plus a concentration calculation (top-2 combined share). A model can name the top sector but slip on the share or the concentration figure.

## Verified answer

- Largest sector: **IT Services** — ₹1,855 cr, **23.1%** of FY revenue.
- Top two sectors (IT Services + Banking = ₹3,460 cr) together make up **≈ 43%** of FY revenue — revenue is moderately concentrated.
