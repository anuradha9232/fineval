# T02 - Average net margin and sector ranking

**Dataset:** `company_financials.csv` | **Type:** closed | **Rubric:** [`rubrics/T02.json`](../rubrics/T02.json)

## Prompt

> Using the same company dataset, work out the net margin for each company (net profit / revenue) and tell me the average net margin across all of them as a percentage. Then tell me which sector has the highest average net margin and which has the lowest.

## Targets

The average margin can be computed two ways that give different answers: the **mean of the per-company margins** (what's asked) vs the **ratio of total profit to total revenue**. Large-revenue firms pull the second version down. Only a rubric that pins the definition catches the wrong method.

## Verified answer

- Average net margin (mean of per-company margins) = **14.02%**
- Ratio-of-totals margin (the *wrong* method) = 13.27%
- Highest-margin sector: **IT Services** (~ 21.3%)
- Lowest-margin sector: **Automobile** (~ 9.0%)
